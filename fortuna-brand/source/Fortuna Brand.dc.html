01  /  Concept and rationale

Fortune, held steady

Fortuna names both wealth and the classical figure of changing circumstance. The product exists because circumstance changes: income arrives, markets move, assets age, obligations mature. Fortuna does not promise to stop the wheel. It gives one person an accurate, calm view of where they stand while it turns.

The identity is therefore built on instruments rather than iconography: a precise monoline mark, a measured type system, a restrained palette drawn from paper, ink and deep cypress. Nothing celebrates. Everything reports.

02  /  Logo

The Crossbar F

An F whose lower arm is carried straight through the stem, the way European currency glyphs are barred. It reads as a letter and as a monetary sign at once, and the crossing sits at the optical centre, acting as a fulcrum: two arms, one balance point. Drawn on a 66-unit grid at a constant 9-unit stroke, entirely straight lines, butt caps, no curves to soften at small sizes. It holds at 16 pixels.

Construction and clear space
Guides fall on the stem at 22, the arm ends at 46 and 54, the bar start at 8, and the three horizontals at 10, 33 and 56. Clear space on all sides equals the stroke width × 1.5 (the dashed frame). Stroke weight never changes relative to the mark. Never re-letter, outline, rotate or add effects.

04  /  Color system

Paper, ink, cypress

One brand hue, one metal accent used at under five percent coverage, and a warm neutral ramp. Financial states are desaturated on purpose: a loss should be legible, not loud. All text pairings meet 4.5:1 against their own surface.

06  /  Spacing, layout, icons, data
4pt spine
{{ s.px }}
Steps of 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64. Card padding 20–24, section gaps 24, page gutters 32 desktop / 20 mobile. Radii: 2 for inputs and cards, 8 for sheets, 0 for tables. Borders 1px, never shadows for hierarchy — only for true overlays.
Icon style
24×24 box, 1.5px monoline, butt caps, miter joins, no fills, no duotone, no rounded corners beyond 1px. Geometry matches the logo grid. Icons are labels, not decoration: one per row maximum.
Data visualisation
Actuals solid, forecast dashed in bronze. Horizontal gridlines only, one hue per series with value encoded by lightness, no 3D, no area stacks beyond three series, always a labelled axis. Currency axes abbreviate (€120k) but tooltips never do.

Voice
State the number, then the context. No exclamation marks, no congratulation, no gamification. Never tell the user how to feel about their money. Insights end in a fact or a date, not encouragement.
Currency and locale
Symbol before the amount, non-breaking space omitted in de-DE. Minor units shown in ledgers and detail views, dropped in summary tiles above €10,000. Minus sign U+2212, never a hyphen. Percentages to one decimal.
