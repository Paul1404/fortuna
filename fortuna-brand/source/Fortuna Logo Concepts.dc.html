Five marks, drawn properly

Same palette and wordmark throughout, so the only variable is the symbol. Each concept is shown large, on the size ladder down to 16 px, on a dark ground, as an app icon and in the horizontal lockup. Tell me a letter and I will apply it across the whole brand package.

2a Ledger

Three rules, right-aligned, shortening as they descend. A page of accounts read as one shape. No letter, no chart, nothing to date.
