# Fortuna symbol — construction

The Crossbar F. Drawn on a 66 × 66 unit grid, constant 9-unit stroke, butt caps, miter joins,
no curves. Three strokes only.

    viewBox   0 0 66 66
    stroke    9 units, butt caps, miter joins, fill none

    stem      M22 10 V56
    upper arm M17.5 10 H54
    crossbar  M8 33 H46

The upper arm starts at x 17.5 rather than at the stem centre so the butt cap closes the corner
flush with the left edge of the stem (22 − 9/2 = 17.5).

The crossbar overshoots the stem on both sides — 14 units left, 24 units right — which is what
makes the mark read as a barred currency glyph rather than as a plain letter F.

    ink bounding box   x 8 → 54, y 5.5 → 60.5
    optical centre     31, 33
    clear space        13.5 units (1.5 × stroke) on all sides
    minimum size       16 × 16 px; at 16 px the stroke is drawn at 10 units for optical weight

Small-size adjustments (16 px only)

    stroke    10 units
    upper arm M17 10 H54

Colour: Azure #0018A8 on light grounds, #6C9BFF on dark grounds, #FFFFFF on azure or navy,
pure black or white for monochrome reproduction. Never re-letter, rotate, outline, skew, or
place the symbol inside a further container shape.
